Research Desk Handbook

Learn the layout

  • Papers is your main workspace for adding, searching, filtering, and reviewing papers.
  • Data is where you import records, export backups, load demo content, and reset the portal.
  • Guide is your built-in help page when you need a quick reminder.
  • The summary cards at the top of Papers show totals for your library, starred items, reading queue, and projects.

Add your first paper

  • Click + Add Paper in the Papers tab to open the paper form.
  • Paste a DOI or DOI URL and use Fetch from DOI to fill in metadata automatically.
  • If you work from PubMed, enter a PubMed ID and use Fetch from PMID.
  • You can also type everything manually and save the paper even without DOI or PMID data.

Keep papers organized

  • Use Project to group papers by topic, experiment, or manuscript.
  • Use Status like To Read, Reading, Reviewed, and Cited to track progress.
  • Use Priority and the star button to keep the most important papers visible.
  • Click a paper title in the table to open its details, notes, tags, and links.

Find what you need fast

  • The search box scans title, authors, journal, project, DOI, PMID, notes, abstract, and tags.
  • Use the filters row to narrow the list by project, year, status, priority, or starred papers.
  • Use Clear filters when you want to get back to the full library quickly.
  • Click any table header to sort the list by title, year, project, priority, dates, and more.

Export, import, and protect your data

  • Select rows in the Papers tab when you want to export only part of your library.
  • Use the Data tab to export a full JSON backup or a CSV file for spreadsheet use.
  • Import JSON to restore a full workspace, or CSV to bring in paper records.
  • Use demo data if you want to explore the interface before adding real papers.

Shortcuts and safety tips

  • Use the ⌘ Shortcuts button in the header any time you want the full keyboard shortcut list.
  • / focuses search, N opens a new paper, and S toggles the starred filter.
  • P jumps to Papers, D jumps to Data, and ? opens the shortcuts panel.
  • Reset All Portal Data clears browser-stored data, so use it only when you really want a clean slate.
